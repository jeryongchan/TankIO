Dirt_Ground081    https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground081   2048x1024, non-square

Downloaded as the 2K-JPG packs. Four maps kept per surface:
  Color         albedo
  NormalGL      OpenGL-convention normals, which is what Unity expects
  Roughness     inverted to smoothness in TankIO/Ground
  Displacement  used only as a height mask for the grass/dirt blend, never for tessellation

Dropped from the packs: NormalDX (wrong handedness for Unity), AmbientOcclusion (little value on
a flat plane under one directional light), and the .blend / .usdc / .mtlx / .tres files.

Dirt_Ground081 is 2:1. Its tiling vector must stay 2:1 or the stones stretch.
